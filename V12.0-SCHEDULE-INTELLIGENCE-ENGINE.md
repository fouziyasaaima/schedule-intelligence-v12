# v12.0 Schedule Intelligence Engine

## Intelligence cockpit
A unified, read-only decision layer aggregates schedule health, target risk, resource pressure, criticality and material task hotspots server-side.

## Large-data principle
Aggregate → identify material signals → explain → drill down. The browser receives compact intelligence summaries rather than recomputing the entire schedule graph.

## Engines connected
- Dependency / CPM signals
- Target protection signals
- Resource pressure
- Monte Carlo forecast summary when available
- Recovery and scenario APIs remain available for action-oriented analysis

## Governance
The cockpit is read-only. Applying a recommendation remains an explicit user action through the existing mutation APIs.
